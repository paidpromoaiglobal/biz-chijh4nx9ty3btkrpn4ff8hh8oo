Our Story

Siddh Laxmi Studio

Siddh Laxmi Studio is a trusted clothing boutique and tailoring studio nestled in Palika Bazar, Sector 9, Malviya Nagar, Jaipur. Run by a passionate husband-and-wife duo, the studio has earned a stellar 5-star reputation for delivering beautifully stitched outfits with flawless fitting. Whether it's a traditional saree blouse, an elegant suit, or a quick alteration, every piece is handled with personal care and expert craftsmanship.

What sets Siddh Laxmi Studio apart is their unwavering commitment to customer satisfaction. Owner Nareshji is known for going the extra mile — extending working hours for urgent orders, accommodating last-minute requests, and ensuring that every garment fits perfectly. From local residents to out-of-town visitors, clients leave not just satisfied, but delighted. With over 262 five-star reviews, Siddh Laxmi Studio stands as one of Jaipur's most beloved tailoring destinations.
